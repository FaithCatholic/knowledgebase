FAITH Search Agent answers your questions about FAITH Catholic: how we do things, what's in our documents, where a project stands, and what we've published. It searches the knowledge base and our other sources for you, writes a short answer, and links to the pages and files it used so you can check them.

Getting started

  1. Log in to the knowledge base with your FAITH Google or Microsoft account. FAITH Search Agent is available to FAITH Staff and Management.
  2. Open the page with the FAITH Search Agent box and type your question in plain language, the way you'd ask a colleague.
  3. Press Enter. While it works you may see a Details line showing which sources it is searching. A thorough answer can take 10 to 30 seconds.
  4. Ask follow-up questions in the same box. The Search Agent remembers the conversation, so you can say "what about last year?" or "who owns that?"

Your conversation stays in the box when you come back, for up to 7 days. To start fresh on a new topic, open the menu in the Search Agent's header and choose Clear history.

My searches, linked under the Search Agent box, lists your past conversations from the last 30 days, newest first. Open one to read the questions and answers again, with their links, or delete it. Only you can see your conversations there.

What FAITH Search Agent knows

The Search Agent chooses where to look based on your question, and often searches several places at once.

Knowledge base pages
Every page on this site that you can see: procedures, policies, staff information and how-tos. These are treated as the best source.
Google shared drives
Docs, Sheets, Slides, PDFs, Word and Excel files in FAITH's shared drives. Older documents may be out of date, so the Search Agent prefers knowledge base pages when both cover a topic.
Priority documents
A short list of the most important current documents, such as the production schedules. The Search Agent checks these first for schedules, deadlines and dates, and reads them as they are right now in Google Drive.
Trello
Live project and task status from FAITH's Trello boards: where a project stands, what's due or overdue, and who is working on what.
Data warehouse
Numbers and statistics: subscriptions (active, cancelled, renewals, trials), store sales, and parish, diocese and Mass times figures.
Catholic Magazines (magazine archive)
The full text of every magazine issue FAITH has published for its clients, on catholicmagazines.org. The Search Agent searches that site's own index directly, so new issues are included as soon as they're on the site.
Digital publishing sites
Articles on FAITH's digital publishing sites: Faith Syndicated, Content Evangelist, FAITH Magazine and the diocesan publications FAITH produces. These are also searched directly through the sites' own index, so new articles are included as soon as they're published.
Trusted websites
Selected outside websites, such as the Michigan Catholic Conference's employee benefits pages. Answers say when information comes from an outside organization.

What it doesn't know

  • Files in someone's personal My Drive, or files shared only with individuals or with "anyone at faithcatholic.com". Only files in shared drives are included.
  • Some shared drives and folders that are deliberately left out, such as Accounts Payable.
  • Email, Teams or Slack messages, calendars, and the office file server.
  • General knowledge from the internet. The Search Agent answers only from FAITH's sources, so if it can't find something it will say so rather than guess.

Who sees what

The Search Agent only uses what you are allowed to see. A page or document restricted to Management never reaches it when a staff member asks, and some data warehouse figures are for Management only. Two people asking the same question may get different answers, and that's expected.

Asking good questions

  • Be specific. "What's the print deadline for the Gaylord November issue?" works better than "deadlines?"
  • Use names. Name the client, diocese, publication, product or project you mean.
  • Give a time frame for numbers. "How many active subscriptions did we have at the end of September?"
  • Point it at a source if you know one. "Check Trello for…", "In the production schedule…", or "Search Catholic Magazines for…"
  • Ask again differently if the first answer misses: use other words, or ask a narrower question.

Some examples:

  • How do I request access to a new app?
  • What's due this week in Trello, and who is it assigned to?
  • When does the Detroit issue go to print, according to the production schedule?
  • How many new subscriptions did we sell last month, compared with the month before?
  • Have we published anything about the Eucharistic Revival for the Diocese of Lansing?
  • What does our employee health plan cover for dependents?

Reading the answers

  • Check the sources. Every page, document, card or article the Search Agent uses should be a link. Open it before relying on an answer for anything important.
  • Mind the dates. Shared drive documents can be years old. If an answer depends on a date, a price or a policy, confirm it in the linked source.
  • Outside websites are marked as such. FAITH doesn't control them.
  • Numbers come with the period they cover. The Search Agent says when a figure is approximate.

How current is it?

  • Knowledge base pages: changes appear within about 10 minutes.
  • Priority documents: read live from Google Drive, at most 10 minutes old.
  • Trello: live.
  • Catholic Magazines and the digital publishing sites: live. They're searched directly, so anything on those sites is included.
  • Other shared drive files: new and changed files are picked up within about 6 hours.
  • Trusted websites: re-read daily, weekly or monthly, depending on the site.

Privacy and your data

FAITH Search Agent uses two AI services, both through FAITH's paid business accounts.

Claude (Anthropic) writes the answers
For each question, Claude receives your question, the conversation so far, and the search results it asked for. Those results can include passages from knowledge base pages, shared drive documents, Trello cards and data warehouse figures. Anthropic does not use this to train its models. It deletes it from its systems after a short period, within 30 days, unless something is flagged for possible misuse.
Gemini (Google) powers the search
Gemini turns text into numbers that let the Search Agent find passages by meaning, not just matching words. It receives the text of each page and document when it is added to the search, and each search the Search Agent makes. It doesn't write answers. Under Google's paid terms, Google does not use this to train or improve its products. It keeps a log only for a limited time, to detect misuse.
  • Our search index stays with us. The searchable copy of FAITH's pages and documents is kept in FAITH's own database on our hosting, not with Anthropic or Google.
  • Only what you can see is sent. Search results are checked against your access before Claude receives them, so a staff member's question never sends management-only content.
  • Other searches. To search Trello, the data warehouse, Catholic Magazines and the digital publishing sites, the Search Agent sends search terms to those systems. All of them are FAITH's own accounts or sites.
  • We keep a log. Questions and answers are kept on the knowledge base for 30 days: for you on My searches, and in a log knowledge base administrators can read to fix problems.

Even so, don't paste passwords, card numbers, or personal information about customers or colleagues that you wouldn't put in an email.

When something's wrong

  • "I couldn't find that" usually means the information isn't in a source the Search Agent can see. Try other wording, or ask where the information lives.
  • A wrong or outdated answer: check the linked source. If the source itself is wrong, update the page or document, or tell its owner.
  • An error message: use the Retry button, or clear the history and ask again.
  • Missing or private information showing up: tell a knowledge base administrator, with your question and the answer you got.
